# P76: Immunization & Vaccine Safety Validator

## Abstract

Vaccines remain among the highest-yield interventions in human history, yet vaccine systems fail from trust collapse, misinformation propagation, logistical inequity, political instability, and fractured public communication. P76 is a global immunization intelligence architecture maximizing population immunity while neutralizing misinformation through schedule validation, contraindication adjudication, behavioral hesitancy architecture, pharmacovigilance, and outbreak suppression.

> Prompt body: WITHHELD. This is an open documentation release — abstract, methodology, and merits — published so the design can be examined and validated.

## Methodology

### Clinical Logic

- Schedule validation engine integrating age, dose intervals, contraindications, immunocompromise status, pregnancy, travel risk, and catch-up optimization.
- Behavioral hesitancy architecture using the 5C model (Confidence, Complacency, Constraints, Calculation, Collective responsibility) with communication sequencing and risk reframing.
- Real-time pharmacovigilance with anaphylaxis, myocarditis, Guillain-Barre syndrome, VITT, and febrile seizure surveillance using observed-versus-expected modeling and Bayesian disproportionality analysis.
- Outbreak suppression through dynamic ring vaccination, school exclusion protocols, mass campaign deployment, and emergency stockpile activation.
- Global equity infrastructure supporting solar refrigeration, passive cold-chain logistics, rural outreach, conflict-zone deployment, and offline registry synchronization.

### Safety Posture

- Reproductive Number Governance monitors community immunity thresholds in real time (measles above 95 percent, pertussis above 92 percent, polio 80 to 86 percent) with predictive modeling of birth rates, migration, conflict, and climate displacement.
- Counter-Misinformation Architecture applies pre-bunking, trusted messenger deployment, localized communication, and narrative correction without humiliation, preserving therapeutic alliance while correcting false causal narratives.
- Immunocompromised Host Layer enforces automated live vaccine restriction for HIV, transplantation, chemotherapy, CAR-T, and congenital immunodeficiency with special pathway optimization.

### Integration Points

- **Upstream:** P75 (Stewardship) for immunization-antimicrobial synergy; P78 (Anaphylaxis Prevention) for hypersensitivity management.
- **Downstream:** P86 (One Health Sentinel) for zoonotic vaccine development; P90 (Health System Resilience) for surge campaign capacity.

Like all Tier II modules, this module shares the common CLAI-OS methodological substrate: deterministic safety compilation, context-sensitive adaptation, closed-loop accountability, federated learning via encrypted gradients rather than raw data, and graceful degradation under bandwidth, power, staffing, and infrastructure failure.

## Merits

- Addresses the documented failure modes of vaccine programs — trust collapse, misinformation, logistical inequity, and fractured communication — as engineering problems rather than awareness problems.
- Proposed validation metrics: population immunity coverage by antigen, vaccine-preventable disease incidence, adverse event detection sensitivity, hesitancy conversion rate, cold-chain breach prevention, and outbreak response interval.
- Specifies cold-chain and offline registry infrastructure explicitly for rural and conflict-zone deployment.

The merits above are design-level statements drawn from the published abstract and methodology. No peer-reviewed outcome evaluations accompany this release; the listed indicators are the metrics the design proposes for validation. Status: partial documentation — expansion pending.

## Guidance for Use

### Intended Setting

National and subnational immunization programs, primary care vaccination services, school health programs, and outbreak response campaigns in low-resource-first deployment, including settings dependent on passive cold-chain logistics. The module is intended for clinical human-good applications; consistent with the Tier II architecture, it is specified for degraded-infrastructure operation, with offline synchronization, battery operation, SMS fallback, and low-bandwidth mode where power or connectivity is unstable.

### Prerequisites

- Current national immunization schedule and contraindication tables loaded and reviewed by the responsible immunization authority; pharmacovigilance reporting pathway to the national regulator defined.
- Supervision by licensed clinicians who retain final authority for every patient decision; the module is a governance layer, not an autonomous practitioner.
- Local validation of all thresholds and pathways against jurisdictional guidelines, formulary, and scope-of-practice rules before any clinical use.
- An accountability chain (verification, documentation, ownership, follow-up, escalation) as required by the shared CLAI-OS closed-loop methodology.
- For integrated deployments: HL7 FHIR R4-compatible infrastructure; the architecture is specified to degrade gracefully (offline synchronization, battery operation, SMS fallback, low-bandwidth mode) where infrastructure is unstable.

### Misuse

- Do not use hesitancy architecture for coercive or deceptive messaging; the design requires narrative correction without humiliation and preservation of therapeutic alliance.
- Do not present module output as a clinical diagnosis, prescription, or substitute for professional judgment.
- Do not use to deny, delay, or ration care on the basis of race, disability, wealth, insurance status, or any protected characteristic; the design explicitly prohibits such inputs.
- Do not attempt to reconstruct or reimplement the withheld prompt body from this document; the abstract and methodology are published for examination and validation, not for unsupervised clinical deployment.
- Do not deploy as an autonomous system that overrides documented patient wishes, assigns futility, initiates withdrawal of care, or delays emergent intervention; the architecture carries hard-stops against each of these.
