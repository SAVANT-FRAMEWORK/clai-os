# P98: Pediatric Cancer & SS Dual-Pathway Navigator

## Abstract

Children simultaneously navigating malignancy and sickle cell disease occupy a distinct biological state requiring independent therapeutic logic rather than adaptation of standard pediatric oncology protocols. P98 integrates pharmacogenomics, tumor lysis prevention, neurovascular protection, fertility preservation, transfusion governance, infection surveillance, psychosocial stabilization, and developmental safeguarding into a unified pediatric systems framework with dynamic arbitration between cure intensity and physiologic tolerance.

## Methodology

**Design objectives**

- Pharmacogenomic hard-stops (TPMT/6-mercaptopurine dose reduction, UGT1A1/irinotecan adaptation, CYP2D6/codeine contraindicated in all children, G6PD/rasburicase validation) to prevent iatrogenic toxicity.
- Tumor lysis syndrome governance via Cairo-Bishop criteria, aggressive hydration, electrolyte surveillance, allopurinol as the standard pathway, and rasburicase only after G6PD validation.
- Neurovascular protection with chronic transfusion support during ALL therapy, transcranial Doppler surveillance, and exchange transfusion escalation for acute risk triggers (hypoxia, dehydration, acidosis, infection, anemia).
- Blood product governance (irradiated, CMV-negative, extended phenotyped, Rh/Kell matched) with alloimmunization prevention and transfusion exposure minimization.
- Fertility preservation (sperm banking above age 12, ovarian tissue cryopreservation as experimental, GnRH agonist protection), psychosocial architecture (school continuity, family burden, trauma surveillance), and long-term survivorship planning (secondary malignancy, iron overload, cardiopulmonary, neurocognitive, transition to adult care).

**Methodological innovation**

- Dual-physiology compiler: every intervention is recalculated against both oncologic efficacy and sickle-cell physiologic survivability in real time, on the principle that standard oncology protocols become hazardous under chronic hemolysis, endothelial dysfunction, vaso-occlusive risk, and alloimmunization burden.
- Triple-vigilance architecture: pharmacologic, hematologic, and neurovascular safety enforced simultaneously rather than sequentially.
- Developmental safeguarding: school continuity, developmental support, and family-centered care embedded into high-intensity therapy workflows.

**Key performance indicators**

- Treatment-related mortality rate
- Stroke prevention during therapy
- Pharmacogenomic hard-stop compliance
- Tumor lysis syndrome incidence
- Fertility preservation completion
- Psychosocial support engagement
- Transition-to-adult care success

**Integration**

- Upstream: P71 (Pediatric Guardian) for general pediatric safety; P91 (Genomic Variant Actionability Arbiter) for pharmacogenomic profiling.
- Downstream: P101 (Psychiatric Oncology & Distress Thermometer) for distress management; P108 (Rehabilitation & Survivorship Architect) for late effects and transition.

## Merits

- Establishes pediatric pharmacogenomic safety infrastructure that prevents dosing catastrophes, integrating TPMT and G6PD safeguards.
- Protects against treatment-induced stroke risk through transfusion governance and neurovascular surveillance.
- Automates fertility-preservation workflows that are routinely missed under treatment pressure.
- Addresses preventable childhood mortality in low-resource settings, where dual-pathway (cancer plus sickle cell) expertise is scarcest.
- Contributes to African genomic medicine infrastructure and child-survival optimization.
