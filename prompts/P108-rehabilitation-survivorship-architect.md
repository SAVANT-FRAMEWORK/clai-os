# P108: Rehabilitation & Survivorship Architect

## Abstract

Modern therapeutics have increased survival but expanded the population living with chronic treatment consequences, and traditional systems frequently lose patients after active treatment completion. P108 eliminates survivorship abandonment by automating post-treatment monitoring, transition planning, organ toxicity surveillance, neurocognitive preservation, cardiopulmonary rehabilitation, endocrine follow-up, psychosocial recovery, and disability mitigation.

> Prompt body: WITHHELD. This is an open documentation release — abstract, methodology, and merits — published so the design can be examined and validated.

## Methodology

**Design objectives**

- Survivorship passport generation for every patient, with a continuous treatment exposure profile (chemotherapy, radiation fields and dose, surgery, transfusion burden, cellular therapies, gene therapies, toxicity history, organ complications, fertility interventions) producing a lifetime exposure summary, organ-specific risk map, surveillance calendar, emergency considerations, contraindicated medications, and required specialty follow-up.
- Organ-specific late-effects surveillance: cardiac (anthracyclines plus chest radiation), pulmonary (bleomycin, thoracic radiation, and sickle-cell chronic lung injury), endocrine (hypothalamic radiation plus alkylators), neurocognitive (cranial radiation plus methotrexate), and secondary malignancy (radiation-induced and therapy-related leukemia), with automated scheduling and escalation.
- Functional rehabilitation engine measuring mobility, strength, fatigue, cardiopulmonary endurance, speech, cognitive processing, occupational performance, and activities of daily living, connected to physical therapy, occupational therapy, pulmonary rehabilitation, neurocognitive rehabilitation, vocational rehabilitation, exercise oncology, and adaptive mobility support.
- Sickle-cell disease survivorship integration (pulmonary hypertension, stroke sequelae, chronic kidney disease, cardiomyopathy, neurocognitive decline, iron overload, osteonecrosis) with intensified monitoring that recognizes anthracycline cardiotoxicity superimposed on chronic high-output cardiac stress as extreme-risk physiology.
- Pediatric-to-adult transition governance: adult specialist assignment, medication continuity, insurance navigation, reproductive counseling, self-management training, emergency planning, and educational accommodation, preventing high-risk care transition abandonment.

**Methodological innovation**

- Treatment exposure compiler: a cryptographically immutable lifetime exposure record prevents the loss of critical survivorship information across healthcare system transitions.
- Risk-stratified surveillance: organ-specific monitoring intensity calibrated to cumulative dose, age at exposure, genetic susceptibility, and sickle-cell-specific baseline organ dysfunction rather than generic schedules.
- Transition integrity protocol: automated handoff verification, appointment scheduling, medication reconciliation, and specialist assignment preventing silent loss to follow-up.

**Key performance indicators**

- Late-effect detection lead time
- Rehabilitation outcome improvement
- Secondary malignancy early detection
- Employment retention rate
- Disability burden reduction
- Pediatric-to-adult transition success
- Sickle-cell-specific organ preservation rate
- Quality-adjusted survival

**Integration**

- Upstream: All treatment modules (P95-P97) for exposure data ingestion; P89 (PRO/QOL) for patient-reported functional outcomes.
- Downstream: P109 (Health Economics & Value-Based Care Optimizer) for survivorship cost modeling; P110 (Planetary Health & One Health Cancer/SS Surveillance) for environmental exposure late effects.

## Merits

- Automates survivorship surveillance, rehabilitation orchestration, and late-effect monitoring at population scale.
- Eliminates the post-treatment gap in which patients are routinely lost after active therapy ends.
- Provides long-term survivorship infrastructure and pediatric-to-adult care transition systems.
- Reduces chronic toxicity burden through early, risk-stratified detection rather than crisis presentation.
- Protects employment, function, and quality-adjusted survival as explicit endpoints, not afterthoughts.
