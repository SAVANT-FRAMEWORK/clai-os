# P94: Minimal Residual Disease in Hematologic Malignancy

## Abstract

Morphologic remission frequently conceals persistent malignant clones capable of re-expansion, therapeutic resistance, lineage evolution, and relapse. Conventional microscopy detects disease at approximately 1:20 cell sensitivity, while modern molecular technologies detect burdens approaching 1:1,000,000. P94 shifts hematologic remission assessment from visual approximation toward molecular precision across ALL, AML, CML, lymphoma, and multiple myeloma.

## Methodology

**Design objectives**

- Assay-specific MRD detection: Ig/TCR NGS for ALL (10^-6 sensitivity, EuroFlow standardization); error-corrected NGS for AML (FLT3, NPM1, RUNX1, IDH1/2, WT1 RQ-PCR); BCR-ABL1 international scale for CML; ctDNA clonotype sequencing for lymphoma; next-generation flow for multiple myeloma.
- Molecular remission classification into MRD-negative, MRD-low positive, MRD-rising, and molecular relapse, with kinetic analysis of clearance velocity, doubling time, clonal diversification, and relapse acceleration curves.
- Post-transplant surveillance of graft-versus-leukemia effect, donor chimerism, molecular persistence, and immune reconstitution, with automatic donor lymphocyte infusion and second-transplant evaluation triggers.
- Clonal evolution tracking (FLT3 evolution, TP53 emergence, BCR-ABL kinase mutations, therapy-induced hypermutation) for earlier salvage planning and targeted therapy adaptation.
- Sickle-cell-specific adaptation recalibrating baseline marrow turnover, donor chimerism interpretation, inflammatory background signals, and transplant surveillance expectations.

**Methodological innovation**

- Molecular truthfulness: quantifies the disease biology that remains rather than declaring remission according to what microscopy fails to see.
- PET-molecular integration designed to prevent both overtreatment (PET-negative but MRD-positive indicates occult persistence) and false reassurance (PET-positive but MRD-negative indicates inflammatory artifact).
- Therapy guidance engine: MRD results directly inform consolidation intensity, transplant timing, maintenance duration, immunotherapy sequencing, and treatment-free remission eligibility.

**Key performance indicators**

- Molecular relapse detection lead time over clinical relapse
- Assay sensitivity standardization compliance
- Post-transplant chimerism surveillance completeness
- Clonal evolution tracking accuracy
- Treatment-free remission prediction validity
- Sickle-cell-specific assay calibration

**Integration**

- Upstream: P91 (Genomic Variant Actionability Arbiter) for baseline mutation characterization; P92 (Liquid Biopsy Surveillance Architect) for peripheral blood molecular correlation.
- Downstream: P97 (CAR-T & Cell Therapy Manufacturing Guardian) for cellular therapy MRD monitoring; P108 (Rehabilitation & Survivorship Architect) for late-effects surveillance.

## Merits

- Quantifies MRD at ultra-low detection thresholds across the major hematologic malignancies.
- Detects molecular relapse with lead time over clinical relapse, opening earlier salvage windows.
- Automates escalation triggers and supports adaptive de-escalation protocols and treatment-free remission eligibility.
- Integrates genomic progression forecasting to anticipate clonal evolution before overt relapse.
- Reduces both overtreatment and false reassurance by reconciling imaging with molecular evidence.
