# P106: Traditional Medicine Integration & Safety

## Abstract

Globally, approximately 80% of populations engage with traditional, herbal, indigenous, or complementary therapeutic systems. Conventional healthcare frequently ignores this reality, creating dangerous undocumented interaction zones between prescribed therapies and unmonitored traditional interventions. P106 operationalizes structured integration through evidence-graded cataloging, herb-drug interaction surveillance, molecular standardization, and culturally embedded healing practice integration.

> Prompt body: WITHHELD. This is an open documentation release — abstract, methodology, and merits — published so the design can be examined and validated.

## Methodology

**Design objectives**

- Evidence stratification framework (systematic review above randomized controlled trial, prospective cohort, case-control, case series, mechanistic preclinical, ethnographic, anecdotal) with continuous updating by clinical efficacy, toxicity, drug interaction probability, product consistency, mechanistic plausibility, and cultural significance.
- Herb-drug interaction surveillance screening critical combinations, including curcumin via CYP3A4 induction with taxane failure risk, St. John's Wort via CYP2D6 induction with tamoxifen failure risk, ginseng with warfarin bleeding risk, grapefruit via CYP3A4 inhibition with toxicity amplification, green tea with bortezomib interaction, and garlic with platelet inhibition.
- Molecular standardization via HPLC-MS fingerprinting, LC-MS metabolomics, DNA barcoding, heavy metal screening, microbial contamination testing, and aflatoxin surveillance, detecting lead, arsenic, mercury, pesticides, industrial adulterants, and steroid contamination.
- Cultural integration framework supporting chaplaincy, Indigenous healer collaboration, guided meditation, traditional nutrition, community rituals, music therapy, hypnosis, and narrative healing where clinically safe, under the explicit principle that cultural respect never overrides physiologic safety.
- Sickle-cell disease-specific traditional medicine guidance (hydration-supportive botanicals, nutritional sorghum formulations, moringa supplementation, shea butter dermatologic support, relaxation therapy for vaso-occlusive crises, guided breathing for pain modulation) with restrictions on iron-containing herbal formulations in transfusion overload, nephrotoxic compounds, unregulated stimulant preparations, and heavy-metal-contaminated products.

**Methodological innovation**

- Evidence-graded integration compiler: neither dismisses traditional medicine as superstition nor accepts it uncritically; the design operationalizes safety-first, evidence-stratified integration that recognizes global healthcare behavior as it is.
- Molecular fingerprinting: HPLC-MS batch consistency verification and contaminant detection to prevent adulteration-related toxicity.
- Cultural safety architecture: respects culturally meaningful healing practices while maintaining pharmacologic accountability through structured documentation and interaction screening.

**Key performance indicators**

- Traditional medicine disclosure rate
- Herb-drug interaction detection rate
- Product contamination identification
- Cultural integration appropriateness
- Sickle-cell-specific traditional medicine safety
- Treatment adherence improvement
- Patient trust index

## Merits

- Integrates evidence-calibrated traditional medicine into clinical governance while screening herb-drug interactions and unsafe combinations.
