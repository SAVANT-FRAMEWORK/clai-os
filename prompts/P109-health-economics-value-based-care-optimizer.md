# P109: Health Economics & Value-Based Care Optimizer

## Abstract

Every healthcare system operates under scarcity constraints, and without explicit economic modeling, allocation decisions default to wealth, geography, institutional bias, or administrative inertia. P109 operationalizes transparent, ethically auditable prioritization frameworks capable of optimizing population health impact while preserving clinical equity and regional adaptability.

> Prompt body: WITHHELD. This is an open documentation release — abstract, methodology, and merits — published so the design can be examined and validated.

## Methodology

**Design objectives**

- Cost-effectiveness modeling (ICER, cost per QALY, cost per DALY averted, net monetary benefit, budget impact, return on investment) with jurisdictional threshold adaptation aligned to frameworks such as NICE and WHO-CHOICE, plus region-specific affordability scaling.
- Value-based payment architecture: bundled payments, outcome-based contracts, risk-sharing agreements, episode-based reimbursement, and population-capitated care, with performance metrics spanning survival, toxicity burden, hospital utilization, functional recovery, and patient-reported outcomes.
- National policy simulation generating five-year budget projections, national implementation plans, workforce requirements, drug procurement forecasts, infrastructure expansion models, and regional disease burden mapping, with scenario modeling for universal hydroxyurea access, national HPV vaccination, expanded radiotherapy, gene therapy deployment, and newborn sickle-cell screening.
- Sickle-cell disease economic modeling comparing lifetime intervention pathways (chronic transfusion, gene therapy, allogeneic transplantation) with indirect costs included: hospitalizations, stroke disability, organ failure, productivity loss, caregiver burden, and educational disruption.
- Ethical allocation governance explicitly rejecting discriminatory rationing by disability, social worth, race, or age, with equity-adjusted allocation modeling and vulnerability-weighting transparency.

**Methodological innovation**

- Transparent rationing compiler: converts hidden inequity into auditable mathematics, on the stated principle that all healthcare systems ration care and ethical systems ration transparently.
- Outcome-linked reimbursement: for example, CAR-T payment contingent on remission sustained at 12 months, comprehensive sickle-cell care bundles designed to reduce vaso-occlusive hospitalization, and oncology pathway adherence incentives.
- Ministry-of-health auto-generation: automated assembly of advocacy briefs, budget impact projections, and policy scenario models for resource-limited decision-makers.

**Key performance indicators**

- Resource allocation efficiency
- Waste reduction
- Treatment sustainability
- National planning accuracy
- Access equity improvement
- Catastrophic expenditure prevention
- Sickle-cell-specific lifetime cost optimization
- Value-based contract success rate

**Integration**

- Upstream: P89 (PRO/QOL) for patient-reported value metrics; P103 (Real-World Evidence & Digital Twin Generator) for synthetic control cost-effectiveness.
- Downstream: P110 (Planetary Health & One Health Cancer/SS Surveillance) for climate-adapted resource planning; all Tier II and Tier III modules for economic feedback.

## Merits

- Integrates cost-effectiveness modeling, DALY optimization, ICER analysis, and ministry-level budget forecasting into one auditable framework.
- Supports national healthcare planning and value-based reimbursement system design.
- Makes resource allocation explicit and reviewable rather than implicit and biased.
- Extends economic modeling to sickle-cell lifetime care pathways, where indirect costs dominate and are routinely ignored.
- Builds catastrophic-expenditure prevention and equity weighting into the model structure itself.
