# P93: Immune Evasion & Microenvironment Profiler

## Abstract

Conventional immuno-oncology relies on single-marker PD-L1 expression, which incompletely predicts response because tumors exist within complex multicellular ecosystems shaped by suppressive cytokines, stromal barriers, metabolic deprivation, vascular dysfunction, and adaptive immune editing. P93 treats immunotherapy response as an emergent systems interaction between malignant cells, stromal architecture, vascular biology, immune infiltration, metabolic signaling, microbiome influence, and temporal therapeutic pressure.

## Methodology

**Design objectives**

- Multiplex immunophenotyping (PD-L1, CD8, CD4, FOXP3, CD163, Granzyme B, TIM-3, LAG-3, TIGIT, CTLA-4, IFN-gamma, MHC-I, VEGF) with spatial analysis via multiplex IHC/IF, CyTOF, and spatial transcriptomics.
- Immune state classification into functional phenotypes: inflamed, immune-excluded, immune-desert, suppressive-myeloid dominant, exhausted T-cell dominant, and hyperinflammatory unstable states.
- Response prediction integrating tumor mutational burden, MSI status, neoantigen load, IFN-gamma signatures, TIDE scores, T-cell receptor clonality, and cytolytic activity indices, avoiding single-marker dependence.
- Immune evasion detection (MHC downregulation, beta-2-microglobulin loss, JAK-STAT disruption, TGF-beta exclusion, WNT activation, VEGF-mediated suppression, metabolic starvation) with hyperprogression risk flagging (MDM2 amplification, EGFR alterations).
- Sickle-cell-specific immunologic recalibration adjusting for chronic IL-6 elevation, endothelial activation, elevated CRP baseline, macrophage activation, functional asplenia, and altered vaccine response.

**Methodological innovation**

- Ecological tumor modeling: tumors are treated as adaptive ecosystems rather than static biomarker states, with continuous recalculation of immune-state evolution during treatment.
- Mechanistic visibility: the design aims to reveal why tumors resist immunity (stromal exclusion, metabolic competition, checkpoint upregulation) rather than merely documenting that resistance occurred.
- Combination rationalization: immune-state classification directs stromal modulation, cytokine targeting, anti-angiogenic integration, vaccine augmentation, and adoptive cellular therapy sequencing.

**Key performance indicators**

- Immunotherapy response prediction accuracy beyond PD-L1
- Futile checkpoint exposure reduction
- Resistance identification lead time
- Rational combination design efficacy
- Sickle-cell inflammatory baseline calibration validity

**Integration**

- Upstream: P92 (Liquid Biopsy Surveillance Architect) for ctDNA immune monitoring; P91 (Genomic Variant Actionability Arbiter) for neoantigen prediction.
- Downstream: P97 (CAR-T & Cell Therapy Manufacturing Guardian) for cellular therapy sequencing; P101 (Psychiatric Oncology & Distress Thermometer) for immune-related adverse event distress.

## Merits

- Classifies tumor microenvironments into actionable functional states rather than relying on single-marker PD-L1 approximation.
- Predicts immunotherapy response using systems immunology rather than isolated biomarkers.
- Forecasts hyperprogression risk before checkpoint exposure in flagged genotypes.
- Integrates cytokine and inflammatory profiles to calibrate therapy selection and combination design.
- Aims to reduce futile checkpoint inhibitor exposure and immunotherapy failure rates.
