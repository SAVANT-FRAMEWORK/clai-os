# P100: Cancer Prevention & Screening Architect

## Abstract

Prevention is the only oncology intervention capable of scaling globally without proportional infrastructure burden escalation. P100 transforms oncology from reactive treatment into longitudinal risk interception by integrating genetic predisposition, environmental exposure, infectious risk, lifestyle patterns, social determinants, regional resource availability, and economic scalability into a continuously adaptive prevention ecosystem.

> Prompt body: WITHHELD. This is an open documentation release — abstract, methodology, and merits — published so the design can be examined and validated.

## Methodology

**Design objectives**

- Population risk stratification engine (average, moderate, high, syndromic/genetic predisposition) with dynamic recalibration by age progression, new diagnoses, exposure accumulation, and behavioral modification success.
- Hereditary cancer surveillance (BRCA1/2, Lynch syndrome, Li-Fraumeni, FAP, MEN, VHL, RB1) with cascade screening, family risk notification, genetic counseling, preventive surgery eligibility, and enhanced imaging surveillance.
- Resource-adaptive screening: mammography/tomosynthesis/MRI for breast; colonoscopy/FIT/FIT-DNA/sigmoidoscopy for colorectal; low-dose CT only for lung (chest radiography explicitly rejected); HPV DNA testing/Pap/VIA for cervical cancer.
- Prevention intervention layer covering smoking cessation, alcohol reduction, weight optimization, physical activity, dietary intervention, UV protection, HPV/HBV vaccination, and occupational exposure mitigation.
- Sickle-cell disease-specific cancer surveillance: renal medullary carcinoma from age 12 with annual urine cytology and renal ultrasound; gallbladder malignancy surveillance in cholelithiasis; colon cancer surveillance in chronic transfusion iron overload.

**Methodological innovation**

- Life-years-saved optimization: screening intensity matched to actual risk while adapting to economic and infrastructural realities across high-resource and constrained environments.
- AI-driven early detection: radiomics integration, risk-adaptive recall systems, missed-screening alerts, longitudinal lesion evolution analysis, and false-positive reduction algorithms.
- Equity and access enforcement: mobile screening deployment, rural outreach, financial toxicity mitigation, community navigator integration, and language access support.

**Key performance indicators**

- Population screening coverage by modality
- Cancer incidence reduction
- Stage shift (early versus late detection)
- Life-years saved per unit of expenditure
- Hereditary cascade screening completion
- Sickle-cell-specific cancer detection rate
- Resource adaptation fidelity

**Integration**

- Upstream: P91 (Genomic Variant Actionability Arbiter) for hereditary risk profiling; P82 (SDOH Integrator) for social determinant-mediated risk.
- Downstream: P89 (PRO/QOL) for screening-related quality of life; P109 (Health Economics & Value-Based Care Optimizer) for cost-effectiveness validation.

## Merits

- Establishes adaptive prevention systems calibrated to regional resources, endemic disease patterns, and population risk stratification.
- Early detection infrastructure designed for stage shift, the strongest population-level survival lever.
- Resource-aware screening logic that remains functional in low- and middle-income country settings rather than assuming high-resource defaults.
- Supports national cancer prevention architectures and public-health optimization.
- Extends surveillance to sickle-cell-associated malignancies that conventional screening programs overlook.
