# P97: CAR-T & Cell Therapy Manufacturing Guardian

## Abstract

CAR-T and advanced cellular therapies are not conventional pharmaceutical products but dynamic biologic ecosystems whose viability, toxicity, expansion kinetics, and therapeutic efficacy are inseparable from manufacturing integrity and host physiology. P97 tracks, validates, predicts, and safeguards cellular therapies from leukapheresis through reinfusion and long-term surveillance via GMP manufacturing controls, immune kinetics, cytokine toxicity prediction, chain-of-identity enforcement, and cryopreservation governance.

## Methodology

**Design objectives**

- Vein-to-vein therapeutic tracking across referral, eligibility, leukapheresis, cell transport, manufacturing, quality release, lymphodepletion, infusion, expansion monitoring, and long-term surveillance, with target intervals (under 21 days commercial, under 14 days academic).
- Manufacturing quality governance with release criteria (transduction efficiency, cell viability, sterility, potency, mycoplasma negativity, replication-competent lentivirus absence) and failure detection (low expansion, contamination, poor viability, insufficient transgene expression, cryopreservation injury).
- CRS/ICANS escalation architecture per ASTCT standardized grading, with tocilizumab 8 mg/kg IV for grade 2 or higher CRS, dexamethasone 10 mg IV every 6 hours for grade 2 or higher ICANS, and hard-stop escalation for rapid ferritin rise, refractory hypotension, status epilepticus, and cerebral edema suspicion.
- Cellular pharmacodynamics governance (B-cell aplasia, CAR copy number, minimal residual disease, antigen escape detection) and failure mechanism tracking (T-cell exhaustion, antigen loss, immunosuppressive microenvironment, poor persistence kinetics).
- Sickle-cell disease considerations (bone marrow dysfunction, inflammatory baseline, endothelial activation, prior alloimmunization, transfusion burden) with apheresis yield optimization, plerixafor mobilization, and vaso-occlusive crisis prevention during collection.

**Methodological innovation**

- Living-medicine lifecycle compiler: every CAR-T infusion is treated as a high-risk immunologic systems event with continuous chain-of-identity, chain-of-custody, and biometric verification from collection to survivorship.
- Toxicity prediction engine: baseline ferritin, CRP, LDH, tumor burden, IL-6, and baseline lymphocyte count integrated into pre-infusion CRS/ICANS risk stratification.
- Long-term surveillance: secondary malignancy monitoring, insertional mutagenesis surveillance, immune dysregulation detection, persistent cytopenia tracking, and hypogammaglobulinemia management.

**Key performance indicators**

- Manufacturing turnaround time
- Quality release pass rate
- CRS/ICANS grade 3 or higher incidence
- Response rate and duration
- CAR persistence kinetics
- Sickle-cell-specific apheresis success rate
- Long-term safety event detection

**Integration**

- Upstream: P94 (Minimal Residual Disease in Hematologic Malignancy) for pre-CAR-T molecular status; P96 (Surgical Oncology Margin & Morbidity Optimizer) for cytoreductive timing.
- Downstream: P104 (Supply Chain & Cold Chain Resilience for Biologics) for biologic transport integrity; P108 (Rehabilitation & Survivorship Architect) for post-cellular-therapy late effects.

## Merits

- End-to-end CAR-T safety orchestration across manufacturing, infusion, and long-term follow-up.
- Standardized CRS/ICANS escalation logic aligned to ASTCT grading with defined pharmacologic pathways.
- Biologic manufacturing governance with explicit release criteria and failure detection.
- Advanced therapy medicinal product infrastructure suitable for both commercial and academic cell therapy programs.
- Sickle-cell-specific collection and mobilization safeguards extend cellular therapy governance to an underserved population.
