## 1. THE SINGULARITY MARK — DESCRIPTION OF THE PROTECTED DEVICE

The Singularity Mark is a graphical device comprising, in combination:

  1.1 A HEXAGONAL OUTER BOUNDARY rendered with nested interior depth —
      concentric hexagonal contours receding toward the center, suggesting
      gravitational compression.

  1.2 BROKEN HORIZONTAL BANDS crossing the hexagon's interior, the breaks in
      the bands encoding the letter "S" — the interruption pattern itself is
      protectable, not merely the letterform.

  1.3 A CENTRAL SINGULARITY EYE at the geometric center of the nested
      hexagons: a point-void motif representing the singularity at which all
      architectural layers converge (L0 Kernel of SPOS v1.0).

  1.4 A CYAN color field (primary specification: cyan on dark ground;
      monochrome cyan variants permitted where reproduction is limited).

The protectable mark is the COMBINATION: hexagonal boundary with nested
depth, broken bands encoding "S", central singularity eye, cyan field. The
combination as a whole, and any substantially similar combination, is
reserved. Generic hexagons, generic eyes, or generic cyan palettes in
isolation are not claimed.
